Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings and tap “Restore Purchases”. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy Premium.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Three Things → Cancel Subscription, or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. The Lifetime purchase is a one-time payment and never renews.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my entries stored?

Only on your device. There is no account, no server and no cloud sync, so nobody else can read your journal. Deleting the app also deletes your entries, so export them first from History → Text or PDF (Premium).

How does the Face ID lock work?

With Premium, go to Settings and turn on “Lock with Face ID”. The app asks for Face ID every time you open it or come back to it. If Face ID fails, you can always use your iPhone passcode.

My daily reminder doesn't arrive

Check that notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings → Three Things → Notifications. You can change the reminder time from the Today screen by tapping “Reminder”.

Deutsch

Käufe wiederherstellen

Öffne die App → Einstellungen → „Käufe wiederherstellen“. Nutze dieselbe Apple-ID wie beim Kauf.

Jahresabo kündigen

iPhone-Einstellungen → dein Name → Abonnements → Three Things → Abo kündigen, oder unter apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Lebenslang ist ein einmaliger Kauf und verlängert sich nicht.

Wo werden meine Einträge gespeichert?

Nur auf deinem Gerät. Kein Konto, keine Server, keine Cloud. Wenn du die App löschst, werden auch deine Einträge gelöscht. Exportiere sie vorher unter Verlauf → Text oder PDF (Premium).
